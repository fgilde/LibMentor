A small addition to your workflow

From your repo.
To your assistant.

  1. Install and initialize.Install the NuGet tool and run libmentor init in your library repository.
  2. Choose your inputs.Review libmentor.json and point it at your docs, declarations and optional metadata.
  3. Generate and publish.Run libmentor generate. Publish the complete output alongside your website, or copy the generated skill into your agent client's skills directory.
libmentor.jsonExample configuration
{
  "schemaVersion": 1,
  "name": "MyLibrary",
  "description": "What your library does.",
  "version": "1.0.0",
  "repository": "https://github.com/owner/MyLibrary",
  "baseUrl": "https://owner.github.io/MyLibrary/ai",
  "output": ".libmentor",
  "documents": ["README.md", "docs/**/*.md"],
  "sources": ["src/**/*.cs"]
}

Grounded in your source

Less guesswork.
More evidence.

LibMentor reuses established parsers, keeps source locations, validates local references and compiles configured example projects. A check command catches generated-output drift in CI.

C# extraction reads source declarations; it is not a complete semantic build or a binary compatibility guarantee. The documentation explains what each input adapter can establish.
